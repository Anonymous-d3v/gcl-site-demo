Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

Green Cycle Limited (GCL) is a Zambian environmental company specialising in pyrogenic carbon capture and storage, Microforestry™, and biochar-based soil solutions. Our registered office is in Zambia.

This policy applies to the website at www.pyrozm.com.

What information we collect

We only collect information you voluntarily give us through our contact form. This includes your name, email address, phone number, and the message you send us.

We do not use cookies. We do not use analytics tools. We do not collect any information passively from visitors.

Why we collect it

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry. This may include answering questions about our products, following up on farm trial requests, or sending a product quote if you asked for one.

We will not contact you for any reason unrelated to what you reached out about.

Who we share it with

Your information is transmitted through Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service that delivers your message to our inbox. Formspree processes your data on our behalf to deliver the message. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third party.

You can review Formspree's own privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your message and contact details for as long as is necessary to handle your enquiry and any follow-up communication. If no business relationship develops, we will not retain your information beyond that correspondence.

Your rights

Under Zambia's Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Request that we delete your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email or social media channels listed on our contact page.

Security

Your information is transmitted over HTTPS. We do not store your data on our website — it goes directly to our inbox via Formspree.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Given the simple nature of this site, we do not anticipate significant changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out through the contact form on this website or find us on Facebook and Instagram — links are on the contact page.
