Our Story

Building permanent value
into the ground.

In 2018, Green Cycle Limited introduced the first commercially available Pyro-Gardeners Biochar to the Zambian market. Since that milestone, we have led the way in turning agricultural waste — maize cobs that would otherwise be burned in fields, wood sanding waste, and poultry litter — into permanent, high-performance soil infrastructure.

More recently, we have pioneered projects focused on tree nursery resilience, demonstrating improved sapling survival and water holding capacity through biochar-amended growing media. We are also conducting preliminary field trials on Pyro Black Urea™, our mechanically fused urea-biochar pellet designed to reduce nitrogen losses and leave recalcitrant carbon in the soil season after season — unlike conventional fertilisers that are fully spent after a single crop.

From post-mining remediation to agribusiness, Green Cycle Limited remains Zambia's reference point for biochar. We do not just sell a product. We build permanent value into the ground.
